## The problem

Staff activity and hours were tracked by hand and collected at the end of each period. Supervisors could not see what happened across the organisation without chasing people.

## What was built

An account-based web app: employees log their daily actions from a phone; supervisors review activity across the organisation, filter by person and period, and see total worked hours on a dashboard.

## How it runs

React + TypeScript front end with shadcn/ui, Supabase for Postgres, authentication and row-level security, deployed as a static site. Design-verification testing before launch and support through the summer.

## Outcome

Adopted by 30+ employees with 1,000+ entries in the first two months. PLAY reported data collection about 50% faster than the manual process.
