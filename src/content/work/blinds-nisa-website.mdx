## The problem

A local blinds shop needed a site that explained its products, captured quote requests, and gave the owner a place to manage inventory and orders.

## What was built

A public website with product pages and a quote-request form, and an internal admin panel (Node.js, Express, MongoDB) for inventory and order management. The marketing site has since moved to WordPress; the quoting workflow grew into [Measure Blinds](/work/measure-blinds).

## Outcome

The site is still the shop's front door, and the relationship led to the full field-estimator build.
